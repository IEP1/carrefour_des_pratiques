White card with a short top accent bar (echoes the logo's flat corner-bar motif) — use to surface a circulaire, dossier, or key instruction inside a longer document or slide.

```jsx
<Callout title="Circulaire du 12 juin 2026" tone="info">
  Dossier de rentrée à transmettre avant le 30 juin.
</Callout>
```

Tones: `info` (blue, default), `warning` (orange), `success` (green) — mirrors the brand's three logo accents. Flat, no gradients.
