Recreates the source deck's recurring L-shaped corner accent bar as a reusable section marker — use at the top of a slide or document section to introduce a new topic.

```jsx
<SectionDivider label="Déroulement de la réunion" color="blue" />
```

Colors map 1:1 to the four accent-bar assets found in the source template (`orange`, `blue`, `green`, `navy`) — copy exact hues from `assets/decorative/`.
